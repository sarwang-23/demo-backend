# Primary sources and methodology boundaries

Reference review date: 2026-10-02. These sources informed data controls; they do not certify this application. No official factor dataset or copyrighted manual is copied into the package.

1. GHG Protocol, Scope 2 Guidance landing page: https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-2-guidance . Defines purchased/acquired electricity, steam, heat and cooling; notes eight instrument quality criteria and the consultation process around the 2015 guidance. The release does not automatically implement consultation proposals.
2. GHG Protocol, Corporate Standard: https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard . Applies to organizational inventories including universities. Its principles require an explicit organizational/operational boundary rather than automatically classifying every university-related activity as Scope 1.
3. GHG Protocol, Scope 2 technical assistance on grid-average fallback: https://ghgptechassistance.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/27680048885652-Can-We-Use-Grid-Average-Emission-Factors-for-Market-Based-Scope-2-in-a-Deregulated-Market-Without-PPAs . Informs the local residual-before-grid fallback control; real supplier and regional availability still require review.
4. GHG Protocol, technical assistance on on-site generation, exports and purchased energy: https://ghgptechassistance.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/37755058002708-If-a-company-generates-renewable-energy-onsite-and-sells-excess-to-the-grid-but-also-purchases-energy-from-grid-can-the-company-still-claim-zero-emissions-from-its-electricity-use-because-of-the-onsite-generation . Informs separation of imports, exports and ownership of energy attributes, rather than an automatic net-zero electricity claim.
5. GHG Protocol, refrigeration/air-conditioning calculation resources: https://ghgptechassistance.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/47400831390356-Refrigeration-and-Air-Conditioning-Equipment . Resource reference, not a promise that all equipment mass-balance situations are covered by one generic form.
6. GHG Protocol, GWP resources: https://ghgptechassistance.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/47400887336724-Global-Warming-Potential-Values . The package requires reviewed versioned GWP inputs rather than inventing an official global-warming-potential table.
7. AASHE STARS Technical Manual resource page: https://stars.aashe.org/resources-support/technical-manual/ . Its academic, engagement, operations and administration coverage informs the university gap map. Our KPI catalog is not the STARS reporting tool, an official scoring implementation or a complete checklist.
8. PostgreSQL row-security documentation: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/ddl-rowsecurity.html . Deployment must use non-owner API roles; owner/superuser access remains privileged. Actual PostgreSQL acceptance is required.

The eight software instrument-review fields are a structured intake aid, not a verbatim reproduction or fully automated assessment of every requirement in the Scope 2 Guidance. A qualified reviewer must document eligibility, vintage/market applicability and the proper generation boundary. Sources may evolve after the delivery date; re-evaluate standards and factors before a new reporting year.
